Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Claude Tracker does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It makes no network requests, has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts.

What the app reads

  • System statistics (CPU, memory, disk, process count) from macOS, to display them.
  • Claude Code session files in the .claude folder in your home folder, read-only, to show session status and recent activity. The App Store edition can read this folder only after you choose it in a file dialog.

Everything it reads is shown on your screen and then discarded. Nothing is written outside the app's own preferences (the launch-at-login setting and the permission to read the folder you chose), and nothing leaves your Mac.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third-party code or services. It is not affiliated with Anthropic.
